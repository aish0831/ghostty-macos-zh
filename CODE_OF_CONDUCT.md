# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a respectful, harassment-free project community.

Be considerate, discuss technical disagreements in good faith, respect different experience levels, and avoid personal attacks, discriminatory language, doxxing, or publication of private data. Maintainers may edit or remove abusive content and restrict participation when necessary.

Report conduct concerns through a private maintainer contact or GitHub's reporting tools. Reports will be reviewed confidentially and fairly.
